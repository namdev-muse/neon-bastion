How to Play

  • Enemies stream along the glowing path toward your core. If one reaches it, the core loses lives. At zero, the map is lost.
  • Click a pad to place the selected tower there. Hover a pad to see how far the selected tower reaches. 1 / 2 / 3 pick Bolt, Pulse or Frost. Click a tower to select it, then U upgrades it and X sells it back for most of its cost.
  • Bolt fires fast single shots at long range. Pulse bursts and hits every enemy near the blast — the swarm answer. Frost chills enemies so your other towers land more hits.
  • Kills pay credits; clearing a wave pays a bonus. Every credit is a decision: a tower now, or save for a stronger one later.
  • Space starts the next wave when you're ready. There is no timer — build at your own pace, then commit.
  • Runners are fast and fragile. Armored enemies shrug off bursts — focus them with bolts. A leak costs core lives: grunts and runners 1, armored 2, bosses 5.
  • Clear a map to unlock the next one. Your best score is saved per map — beat it.

Controls

  • Click / tap: select pad or tower · 1 / 2 / 3: pick tower type
  • U: upgrade · X: sell · Space: start wave · F: speed
  • P: pause · M: mute · R: restart · Esc: back
